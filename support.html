Common questions

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.
How do I cancel my subscription?
In the app, Settings › Manage Subscription, or in the iPhone Settings app › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.
Can I change how many new kanji I get?
Yes, Settings › New items per day. Free gives 3 or 5 a day; Pro allows 10, 15 or 20.
The handwriting check says my stroke is wrong.
Start each stroke where the red dot shows and draw it in one motion. Tap Hint to see the next stroke.
How do I add the widgets?
Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, and choose Kanji Bento.
I found a mistake in a sentence.
Thank you! Email the kanji and the sentence and we'll fix it in the next update.
