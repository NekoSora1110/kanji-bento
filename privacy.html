Privacy Policy

Kanji Bento · Last updated November 7, 2026

Short version: Kanji Bento does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What you study stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your study progress (which kanji you learned, review dates, daily history, study lists) and your settings are stored only on your device.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you have Kanji Bento Pro and turn on iCloud sync, your progress is stored in your own private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices. Apple handles this storage under its own privacy policy. We cannot see it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether Pro is active.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Speech and handwriting

Japanese audio is spoken by your device's built-in voice. Handwriting is checked on your device and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Children

Kanji Bento does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
